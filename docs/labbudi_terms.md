## 1. Introduction

LabBudi is a free, interactive science laboratory that runs in a web browser. Students can try virtual experiments, handle virtual equipment, make observations and learn how real practicals work, all through play.

LabBudi was created and founded by Shreya Challagali. It is designed for students aged 11 to 14 (Year 7 to Year 9), and its practicals follow the iGCSE science curriculum. It is available in English, Mandarin, Korean, Bahasa Malaysia and Hindi.

These terms explain how LabBudi can be used, what LabBudi does and does not collect, and what belongs to whom. They are written to be read by students, parents, teachers and schools.

The short version:

- LabBudi is free, with no sign-up and no adverts.
- LabBudi does not collect personal information.
- Teachers and schools are welcome to share and use it in lessons.
- The content belongs to LabBudi and its creator, so please do not copy it or sell it.

## 4. What LabBudi Does Not Collect

LabBudi is built to respect the privacy of its users, especially children. This section is the most important one for parents and schools.

LabBudi does **not** collect:

- Any personal information.
- Names, email addresses, ages or locations.
- Accounts, logins or passwords. There is no account system at all.

LabBudi also does **not**:

- Set its own cookies, beyond what a browser needs to display the page.
- Use its own tracking tools or analytics. If anonymous analytics are ever added in the future, they will not identify any person, and these terms will be updated to say so.
- Sell, rent or share data with any third party, under any circumstances.
- Show advertising.

**Content from other services:** a few pages show pictures or designs that come from other services, for example the artwork on the Our Mission page and some of the photographs in the "What does it mean?" slides. These load straight into the browser from those services, and they may collect basic technical information (such as an IP address) under their own policies. LabBudi does not control this and does not receive that information.

**How progress is saved:** when a student completes part of a practical, LabBudi saves their progress in their own browser using a feature called localStorage. This information stays on the student's own device. It is never sent to a server and LabBudi cannot see it. Clearing the browser's data, or using a different browser or device, will remove the saved progress.
